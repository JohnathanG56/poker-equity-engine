How it works

  • All hands known: exact enumeration of every possible board, zero error.
  • Ranges / random: Monte Carlo in growing batches; bars update live and the ± shows one standard error.
  • Range syntax: QQ+, AKs, ATo+, 76s, KQ, 22-55, A2s-A5s
